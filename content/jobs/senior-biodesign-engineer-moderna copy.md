Moderna Therapeutics is seeking a Senior Biodesign Engineer to join our innovative team developing next-generation therapeutic solutions using synthetic biology and advanced materials design.

## About the Role

As a Senior Biodesign Engineer, you will lead the development of novel biomaterial platforms for drug delivery and tissue engineering applications. You will work at the intersection of synthetic biology, materials science, and medical device development to create groundbreaking therapeutic solutions.

## Requirements

- Ph.D. in Bioengineering, Materials Science, or related field with 5+ years of industry experience
- Extensive experience in biomaterials design and characterization
- Strong background in synthetic biology and protein engineering
- Proven track record of leading cross-functional technical teams
- Experience with medical device development and regulatory requirements
- Excellence in experimental design and data analysis
- Strong publication record and patent portfolio

## Responsibilities

- Lead the development of novel biomaterial platforms for therapeutic applications
- Design and execute experiments to characterize new materials and biological systems
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate biological and materials components
- Mentor junior engineers and scientists
- Author technical documentation and regulatory submissions
- Contribute to intellectual property development
- Present research findings to internal and external stakeholders

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and equity package
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
- 401(k) with generous company match
- Flexible work arrangements
- Professional development opportunities
- On-site fitness center and cafeteria
- Paid parental leave
- Annual conference and training budget

## Why Moderna?

At Moderna, we believe that innovative thinking and cutting-edge technology can revolutionize medicine. Our mRNA platform has already transformed vaccine development, and we're just getting started. Join us in developing the next generation of therapeutic solutions that combine synthetic biology with advanced materials design.

## Location

This position is based at our headquarters in Cambridge, Massachusetts, a global hub for biotechnology innovation. The role offers opportunities for collaboration with leading academic institutions and biotech companies in the greater Boston area.
